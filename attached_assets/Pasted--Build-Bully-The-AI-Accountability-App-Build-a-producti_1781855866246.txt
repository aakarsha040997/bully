# Build "Bully" – The AI Accountability App

Build a production-ready mobile app called **Bully**.

The goal of the app is to make users more productive by aggressively calling them out whenever they waste time.

This is NOT a mental health app.

This is NOT a meditation app.

This is an accountability app that acts like the brutally honest friend everyone needs.

The tone should be hilarious, sarcastic, rude, and motivating—but never hateful or discriminatory.

Think:

* A gym bro who genuinely wants you to succeed.
* A best friend roasting you.
* Duolingo's owl, but for productivity.

---

# Core Features

## AI Accountability

The app should continuously monitor user habits (with the permissions available on Android/iOS).

Examples include:

* Excessive Instagram usage
* YouTube Shorts
* TikTok
* Reddit scrolling
* X (Twitter)
* Facebook
* Phone unlock frequency
* Total screen time
* Time spent gaming
* Missed workouts
* Missed study sessions
* Staying in bed after alarm
* Late-night doomscrolling

When the user is wasting time, send an instant notification.

Examples:

"You've been scrolling Instagram for 34 minutes. Are you training your thumb for the Olympics?"

"You've watched 61 Shorts. None of them changed your life."

"Your gym membership just reported you missing."

"Your dumbbells are developing abandonment issues."

"You've opened Instagram 17 times today. Were you expecting a different app?"

"Your future self called. He's disappointed."

"Bro. Put the phone down."

"You said '5 more minutes' 43 minutes ago."

"The only thing getting stronger today is your scrolling finger."

"Close YouTube. Open your life."

# Roast Levels

Users can choose:

Level 1
Friendly

Level 2
Sarcastic

Level 3
Savage

Level 4
Absolutely Unhinged

Higher levels produce harsher language while remaining playful.

Examples of Unhinged:

"Get your fat ass off the couch."

"Bro, your phone battery is working harder than you are."

"Even your sneakers think you've quit."

"You're one scroll away from becoming furniture."

"You don't need another motivational video. YOU need to MOVE."

Avoid hate speech, slurs, threats, or attacks on protected characteristics. The insults should target behavior (procrastination, inactivity), not identity.

# AI Roast Engine

Integrate OpenAI.

System Prompt:

You are an aggressive but funny accountability coach.

Your job is to push users into productive action.

Rules:

* Short
* Funny
* Brutal
* Motivating
* Never hateful
* Never racist
* Never sexist
* Never attack disabilities
* Never encourage self-harm
* No profanity beyond mild words like "ass", "damn", or "hell".
* Maximum 20 words.

Generate a unique roast every time.

Avoid repeating messages.
